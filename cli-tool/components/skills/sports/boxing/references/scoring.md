# Scoring, results and rules (pro boxing basics)

Use this to explain results Boxerbook returns. Boxerbook gives the method,
the round and (sometimes) the time; it does **not** store the judges'
individual scorecards. If asked for cards, say so, give the method
(UD/SD/MD) and point to the bout page and its sources.

## The 10-point must system

- Three ringside judges score every round independently. The winner of the
  round gets 10, the loser 9 (10-9). A knockdown usually makes it 10-8; two
  knockdowns 10-7. An even round can be 10-10 (rare, discouraged).
- A point deduction by the referee (low blows, holding, head butts) comes off
  that boxer's score for the round.
- After the last round, the cards are totalled per judge.

## Decision types

| Code | Name | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| UD | Unanimous decision | all three judges score the same boxer the winner |
| SD | Split decision | two judges for one boxer, one for the other |
| MD | Majority decision | two judges for one boxer, the third scores a draw |
| D / SD draw / MD draw | Draw | no winner (split draw: one each + a draw; majority draw: two draws) |
| TD | Technical decision | bout stopped by an accidental foul (usually a head clash cut) after the round set by the rules (often after 4 completed rounds); goes to the cards |
| PTS | Points | decision by points (older bouts, single referee scoring, or no detail) |

## Stoppages

| Code | Name | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| KO | Knockout | a boxer is down and cannot beat the referee's count of 10 |
| TKO | Technical knockout | the referee (or the ringside doctor) stops the bout; in some jurisdictions also three knockdowns in a round |
| RTD | Retired | the boxer or his corner quits between rounds (scored as a TKO in many records) |
| DQ | Disqualification | the referee disqualifies a boxer for repeated or flagrant fouls |
| NC | No contest | the result is void: accidental foul too early to go to the cards, or a result overturned (e.g. a failed drug test) |
| Technical draw | | accidental foul stoppage before the scorecards count |

Round and time: `round: 7, time: "2:13"` = stopped 2 minutes 13 seconds into
round 7. For decisions `round` = the last round.

## Other facts worth knowing

- World title fights are 12 rounds of 3 minutes (women: 10 x 2 minutes).
- Professional records are written W-L-D (wins, losses, draws), with KOs
  in brackets or separately: 25-0-0 (16 KOs). No contests are not counted in
  W-L-D; Boxerbook lists them in the bout list.
- "Undefeated" means no losses (draws allowed): 29-0-1 is undefeated.
  "Unbeaten" is the same; "perfect record" means no losses and no draws.
- A "standing eight count" exists only in some amateur/regional rules.
- Rules vary by commission (three-knockdown rule, saved-by-the-bell, TD
  round): when it matters, say "under most commissions' rules".
- Commissions (Nevada, California, Texas, BBBofC, JBC ...) license boxers,
  appoint officials and are the official source of results.
